# Spec Delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Section placement and visibility
The landing page SHALL show the swipe video section directly below the info section, in every site phase. The photo field section (per `photo-field`) SHALL render directly below it, and in Phase 3 the gallery teaser SHALL follow the photo field.

#### Scenario: Any phase
- **WHEN** a visitor opens the landing page in Phase 1, 2 or 3
- **THEN** the swipe video section appears directly below the info section, with the photo field directly below it
