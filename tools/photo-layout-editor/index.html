Photo field layout

Drag photos to move them. Drag the round handle to resize. The dashed box is one copy of the pattern, which repeats in every direction; the faded photos around it show how its edges join up.

Photos

Click a photo to add it to the middle of the view. The number shows how many times it's placed (red = not placed).

Selected photo

Click a photo in the layout to select it.

Keys: arrows nudge 1px (Shift = 10px), [ ] change layer, Delete removes, Ctrl+Z undoes.

Pattern size

400px wide is about a phone screen. Photos are shown at this size on phones, and larger on bigger screens.
